      How the colors and data work

      Green: all checked criteria fit. Yellow: at least one is just outside a limit, with no clear miss. Orange: at least one fit or near fit and one clear miss. Red: all checked criteria clearly miss. Hollow pins are provisional: one or more active criteria are missing. A solid gray pin means none could be checked. With no active criteria, pins have a neutral gray outline. Near allowances: 10% for cost and rain; 2 °C for temperature; 1 hour for time zone.

      Social and mosquito ratings are coarse, scoped to their stated sources where available, and never guessed from city population or climate. Unavailable observations remain unknown. Flight search opens Google Flights. WhereNow does not check prices or use them to rank destinations.
