    How the colors and data work

    Green: all active criteria fit. Yellow: all criteria are close enough, with at least one just outside a limit. Orange: at least one known fit or near fit plus at least one clear miss. Red: all known criteria clearly miss. Gray: no criteria selected, or missing evidence means the destination cannot be fully classified, unless a mix of fits and misses is already established. Near allowances: 10% for cost, rain and airfare; 2 °C for temperature; 1 hour for time zone.

    Social and mosquito ratings are coarse, scoped to their stated sources where available, and never guessed from city population or climate. Unavailable observations remain unknown. Flight quotes are added by you and tied to route, direction, dates and month, not bookings or current market estimates. Search links send route details to the provider only when clicked.
